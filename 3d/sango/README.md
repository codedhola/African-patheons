# Ṣàngó: realistic render notes

A photoreal Ṣàngó made after the Òrìṣà and Alụsị gallery. The page has a hero image, a 36-view turntable, five close-ups and a sources section: https://claude.ai/artifact/JmahtF1kFYrdWYvpxcZ5GH

## The render's own choices (not from sources)

- His face, build and short beard.
- The crown's motifs: zigzag lightning, a white oṣé emblem, the bird's shape. The veil is cut short so the face shows.
- The embroidery: spirals and an oṣé inside a teardrop panel on the chest, in white thread.
- The cloth's scale: strips 10.5 cm wide with two woven white squares across each strip, each square with a red centre.
- The pose and setting: arm raised as on the Enwonwu statue, plus a storm with distant lightning.
- The làbà's red body and the white on its flap and fringe (the source names red and yellow dyes only).
- Brass bangles and bead bracelets at the wrists.

## How it was made

Blender Cycles. The body is MakeHuman's CC0 base mesh and rig from [MPFB2](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2). The agbádá is one simulated cloth pattern of 14,219 vertices, draped with the arms out and then simulated into the pose. The crown has 15,532 modelled beads. There are 133,193 hair curves: beard 86,951, scalp 43,397, brows 2,515, lashes 330.
